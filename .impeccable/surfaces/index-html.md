# Surface brief: / (Tier board)

Scope: one page tier board plus data snapshot and fetch script. Mode: Operate.

Audience and job: a model tracker opens the page, reads the top tiers at a glance, taps a lens chip to re-cut the board, and trusts the edition stamp for freshness.

Action: scan S tier first, switch lens with one tap, hover or tap a tile for detail.

Proof and content: 28-model Artificial Analysis snapshot, six lenses, score-band tier rows, real company logos, AA company inks, edition date on the masthead.

Constraints: Radix ink scale; fixed tier legend across lenses; chips own the action color; tabular numerals; full board usable at 390px; motion honors reduced-motion; whole board fits near one screen.

Chosen direction: The Season Annual (ruled rank tables; each refresh prints a dated edition). Recomposition: classic tier rows replace score rows; tiles carry logo plus name; detail moves to a tooltip card.

Memorable moment: a tile hover lifts an ink detail card; a chip tap ripples the tiles into the new lens.

Unresolved: none. Chart engine decided as custom tiles (TanStack stayed a motion reference). Tier cuts decided as snapped score bands.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The board is a season annual, not a table: tier rows lettered S down, each refresh a dated edition, tiles as the rank squares. It refuses the dense sortable grid and the neon card wall.

OWN-WORLD: Fog paper ground with ink hairline rules on the Radix scale; condensed grotesk heads with tabular numeral detail; tier letters as tinted label cells in fixed legend inks; tiles on faint maker tints with no borders; chips carry the sole action color; detail card in ink.

STORY: The visitor learns the winners in one glance, believes the edition stamp, and taps chips to compare lenses without loss of place.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Edition masthead with snapshot date left and tier legend right; six lens chips as index tabs; S and A tile rows fully visible with logo plus name per square; band range on each label cell.

FORM: Assigned number 7 of 7 grounded (tower, fight tiers, transit board, console, terminal, medal table, annual); seed cdaf1e52; user locked assigned over pick and two competitive challengers. Recomposition per user ask keeps the world and swaps rows for classic tiles. Signature interaction: tile tooltip card plus edition change ripple, tiles crossfade in a top-down stagger.
